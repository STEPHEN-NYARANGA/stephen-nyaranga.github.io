About

A diligent scientist driven by curiosity and real-world impact.

I am a climate scientist and geophysicist doing a PhD in Climate Science as a CLARITY-Africa Fellow. CLARITY-Africa is coordinated by Imperial College London, and my fellowship is awarded through the African Institute for Mathematical Sciences, Research and Innovation Centre (AIMS-RIC) in Kigali, Rwanda. I am co-supervised at Imperial, and I am registered at Jomo Kenyatta University of Agriculture and Technology (JKUAT) for a PhD in GIS and Remote Sensing. My research combines deep learning and radar (SAR) analysis to map floods and strengthen urban resilience in Rwanda.

I am passionate about using satellite technology to solve real-world problems. Radar (SAR), interferometric radar (InSAR), optical imagery and GNSS let us measure floods, ground movement and changes on the land across whole regions, again and again. I want to put these tools to work in disaster risk reduction and early warning, agriculture, energy and water resources, among other areas.

My academic journey spans four countries. I hold a BSc with First Class Honours from Karatina University, an MSc with Distinction from AIMS-Senegal, and pursued an MSc in Geophysics at ICTP-EAIFR.

I am a member of the AXIOM Research Group at AIMS-RIC (for which I built the website), and have hands-on experience in IT leadership, web development, teaching, and digital transformation.

Research Interests

Satellite technology, including radar (SAR), InSAR, optical imagery and GNSS, applied to practical problems.

“The Earth of wonders that is alive.”

Stephen Nyaranga

Disasters & Early Warning

Mapping floods with Sentinel-1 radar and measuring ground deformation from earthquakes, volcanoes and landslides with InSAR, to support early warning and response.

Agriculture & Food Security

Monitoring crops, soil moisture and land use with radar and optical time series, to give farmers and planners timely information.

Energy

Tracking ground movement at geothermal fields and energy infrastructure with InSAR. My MSc thesis used Sentinel-1 InSAR to study deformation at Kenya's Olkaria geothermal field (2020–2024).

Climate, Water & Cities

Combining Earth observation with AI to study floods, water resources and urban resilience, and to strengthen weather and climate services in Africa.

CLARITY-Africa PhD in Climate Science, coordinated by Imperial College London and awarded through AIMS-RIC; registered at JKUAT for a PhD in GIS and Remote Sensing. Multi-sensor deep learning framework combining SAR time-series, optical EO, and terrain metrics.

Current · Fieldwork

Field Campaign for Flood Validation Data

Rwanda · Apr–May 2026

Satellite flood maps need ground truth. In April and May 2026 I worked with the team to record flood marks on buildings, set up measurement equipment and install sensors at river crossings. These observations will be used to validate the Sentinel-1 flood extents and to calibrate flood-depth estimates in my PhD work.
