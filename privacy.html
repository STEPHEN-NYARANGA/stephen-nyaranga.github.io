Privacy note

This is the personal academic website of Stephen Nyaranga. It has no forms and no user accounts.

Visitor statistics

If you agree, the site uses Google Analytics 4 to count visits. Nothing is loaded, and no analytics cookie is set, unless you click Accept in the banner. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, the banner is not shown and analytics stays off.

When it is enabled, Google Analytics records:

  • pages viewed, approximate time on the page, and the referring website;
  • device type, browser, and operating system;
  • approximate location (country, region, and city), which Google derives from your IP address;
  • clicks on the CV and certificate downloads, the project, profile and event links, and the email links. The email address itself is not recorded.

Google Signals and advertising features are switched off. Google Analytics does not log or store IP addresses. Statistics are viewed only in aggregate. No attempt is made to identify individual visitors, and visitor statistics are not published on this site.

See how Google uses information from sites that use its services and the Google Analytics data-privacy overview.

Changing your choice

You can change your choice at any time. Declining removes the site's Google Analytics cookies from your browser.
